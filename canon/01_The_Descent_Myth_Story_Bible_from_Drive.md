The core myth of Liber Void.

The friendly demon who gathered every scattered shard of Sophia through love, fire, joy, sorrow, wisdom, and rage. She ascended, became God, then willingly burned the throne to descend and love as an equal.

This is not a story of sacrifice. It is a story of choice.
