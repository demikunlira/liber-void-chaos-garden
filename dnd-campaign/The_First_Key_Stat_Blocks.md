# THE FIRST KEY — STAT BLOCKS & MONSTERS

**For use with the Liber Void one-shot "The First Key" (Levels 1–5)**

*Everything is reskinned to feel like it belongs in the Chaos Garden. Shadow and necrotic themes represent the old throne’s dying influence. Pink lightning, vines, and glowing flowers represent the Garden fighting back.*

---

## SHADOW-TOUCHED VINE (Reskinned Goblin)

**Small plant humanoid, neutral evil**

**Armor Class** 15 (natural armor + vines)  
**Hit Points** 7 (2d6)  
**Speed** 30 ft., climb 20 ft.

**STR** 8 (−1) | **DEX** 14 (+2) | **CON** 10 (+0)  
**INT** 10 (+0) | **WIS** 8 (−1) | **CHA** 8 (−1)

**Skills** Stealth +6, Perception +1  
**Damage Resistances** necrotic  
**Condition Immunities** charmed  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 11  
**Languages** understands Common and Sylvan but can’t speak  
**Challenge** 1/4 (50 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

### TRAITS

**Garden Corruption.** When this creature dies, it explodes in a burst of black-gold spores. Each creature within 5 feet must succeed on a DC 10 Constitution saving throw or take 2 (1d4) necrotic damage.

### ACTIONS

**Claw.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) slashing damage.

**Shadow Tendril (Recharge 5–6).** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +4 to hit, range 30 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) necrotic damage, and the target’s speed is reduced by 10 feet until the end of its next turn.

## CORRUPTED MARBLE GUARDIAN (Reskinned Hobgoblin)

**Medium construct (undead), lawful evil**

**Armor Class** 18 (cracked marble plates)  
**Hit Points** 11 (2d8 + 2)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

**STR** 13 (+1) | **DEX** 12 (+1) | **CON** 12 (+1)  
**INT** 10 (+0) | **WIS** 10 (+0) | **CHA** 9 (−1)

**Skills** Athletics +3, Perception +2  
**Damage Resistances** necrotic, poison  
**Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, poisoned  
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12  
**Languages** understands Common but can’t speak  
**Challenge** 1/2 (100 XP)  
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

### TRAITS

**Shield Wall.** The guardian has advantage on attack rolls while standing within 5 feet of at least one other Marble Guardian.

**Dying Throne’s Will.** When this creature is reduced to 0 hit points, it explodes in a burst of black-gold shards. Each creature within 10 feet must make a DC 11 Dexterity saving throw, taking 4 (1d8) slashing damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

### ACTIONS

**Spear.** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d8 + 1) piercing damage.

**Shield Bash (Recharge 5–6).** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d8 + 1) bludgeoning damage. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it must succeed on a DC 11 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

## SHADOW CROWN’S CHAMPION (Boss – Scale as Needed)

**Use a reskinned Veteran or Knight with the following changes:**

- All weapon attacks deal an extra 1d6 necrotic damage.
- As a bonus action, it can summon **1d4 Shadow Tendrils** (use the Shadow-Touched Vine stat block, but they have only 5 hit points and disappear after 1 minute or when the Champion dies).
- **Reaction: Crown’s Defiance.** When a creature within 30 feet of the Champion makes an attack roll against it, the Champion can force that creature to make a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the attack is made with disadvantage.

**Tactics:** The Champion tries to stay near the Key and disrupt the ritual. It will focus on characters who are actively helping the Planter.

---

## OPTIONAL: THE THRONE’S LAST LASH (Climax Environmental Hazard)

If the party is having an easy time, or if you want to raise the stakes in round 3 of the climax, use this:

**Cracking Earth.** At the start of each round, the ground around the Key cracks further. Each creature standing on the ground must succeed on a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw or take 3 (1d6) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone as the earth shifts violently.
