This folder holds the complete, playable foundation of the Liber Void D&D campaign.

### Structure

- **Liber_Void_DnD_Campaign_Overview.md** — Full campaign bible and Tier structure
- **The_First_Key_One_Shot.md** — Complete Tier 1 prologue one-shot (Levels 1–5)
- **The_Cracked_Crown_Hall_Dungeon.md** — Detailed 6-room mini-dungeon with investigation and big thematic monsters
- **The_First_Key_Stat_Blocks.md** — Fully themed Garden vs Old Throne stat blocks
- **The_First_Key_Pregenerated_Characters.md** — Four ready-to-play shards with deep Garden ties
- **Liber_Void_Garden_Shops_and_NPCs.md** — Living shops, merchants, and items with roleplay weight
- **The_Planting_Ritual.md** — Sovereign ceremony for the First Key and any new planting

Everything here is designed to feel alive, intimate, and true to the descent: pink lightning, choice over destiny, the Garden that remembers, and the burning of thrones.

Further dungeons, bestiary expansions, and Tier 2+ material will continue to root here as the campaign grows.
