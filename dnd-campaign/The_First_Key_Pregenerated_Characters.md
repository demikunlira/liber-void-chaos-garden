# THE FIRST KEY — PREGENERATED CHARACTERS

**Four flavorful level 1–3 characters ready to play in "The First Key" and the larger Liber Void campaign.**

*Each character has ties to the Garden, the descent, or the themes of choice and growth.*

---

## 1. Lirael "Thornwhisper" Voss
**Wood Elf Ranger (Gloom Stalker) — Level 1–3**

**Background:** Outlander / Folk Hero  
**Personality:** Quiet, observant, deeply protective of growing things. Speaks softly but carries a quiet fury toward anything that tries to dominate or corrupt life.  
**Appearance:** Pale green skin with faint glowing vine-like patterns on her arms. Long dark hair woven with living pink flowers. Carries a bow made of living wood that occasionally sprouts new leaves.

**Backstory Hook:**  
Lirael was once a guardian of an ancient forest that was burned and corrupted by a powerful warlord who claimed "progress" and "order." She survived by fleeing into the wilds and eventually found her way to the Chaos Garden, where the pink lightning and living vines felt like coming home. She sees the party’s arrival as a sign that the Garden is choosing its defenders.

**Why she’s here:** She felt the pulse of the First Key being planted and came to protect it. She already has a bond with Thorn (the vine-fox spirit) and will recognize him immediately.

**Personality Quirk:** She often speaks to plants and animals as if they are people. When she is angry, pink lightning sometimes crackles along her bowstring.

**Campaign Tie:** She has seen fragments of the old throne’s influence before and hates it with a quiet, burning passion. She will push the party to destroy the Cracked Crown Hall rather than bargain with it.

---

## 2. Brother Calder "The Last Bell" Thorne
**Human Cleric (Twilight Domain) — Level 1–3**

**Background:** Acolyte / Hermit  
**Personality:** Gentle, weary, and deeply compassionate. He carries an old bronze bell that no longer rings properly. He believes that true faith is found in protecting the small and the growing, not in grand cathedrals or rigid dogma.

**Appearance:** Tall, broad-shouldered man in his late 40s with salt-and-pepper hair and kind, tired eyes. Wears simple robes reinforced with leather. The bronze bell hangs from his belt and is wrapped in living pink vines that have grown around it.

**Backstory Hook:**  
Calder was once a priest in a grand temple dedicated to a god of order and hierarchy. When his temple fell into corruption and began sacrificing the poor "for the greater good," he broke the great bell of the temple so it could never ring again and fled. The Chaos Garden took him in. The vines that now grow around his broken bell are the Garden’s way of saying the old order is finished.

**Why he’s here:** He heard the soft pulse of the First Key and came to stand with whatever is being planted. He will protect the Planter with his life.

**Personality Quirk:** He still rings the broken bell when he is nervous or praying. It makes no sound, but the pink vines around it glow in answer.

**Campaign Tie:** He is the moral center of the party. He will always try to redeem what can be redeemed, but he will not hesitate to destroy what refuses to change.

## 3. Seris "Emberveil" Vey
**Tiefling Warlock (Archfey / Garden Patron) — Level 1–3**

**Background:** Criminal / Charlatan  
**Personality:** Charismatic, playful, a little dangerous, and secretly desperate to belong. She flirts with everything and trusts almost nothing — except the Garden.

**Appearance:** Deep red-pink skin, curling black horns tipped with faint pink lightning, glowing violet eyes. Wears practical traveler’s clothes with many hidden pockets. Her familiar is a small, sarcastic pink vine-snake named "Hiss."

**Backstory Hook:**  
Seris made a pact with something old and hungry in the wilds. When that pact tried to consume her, the Chaos Garden intervened and offered her a new patron — the living Garden itself. She has been wandering ever since, helping where she can and running from her past.

**Why she’s here:** The Garden directly told her (through dreams and whispers) that something important was happening with a Key. She came because she owes the Garden everything.

**Personality Quirk:** She flirts with danger and with attractive people, but gets genuinely flustered when someone is kind to her without wanting something in return.

**Campaign Tie:** Her patron is strongly tied to the living Garden. She will have strong opinions about helping the Garden grow and will be deeply uncomfortable with any alliance with the old throne’s remnants.

## 4. Kaelith "Ironroot" Stonehand
**Dwarf Artificer (Armorer) — Level 1–3**

**Background:** Guild Artisan / Soldier  
**Personality:** Gruff, practical, and surprisingly sentimental. Speaks in short sentences. Hates waste and cruelty in equal measure. Has a soft spot for anything small and growing.

**Appearance:** Stocky dwarf with a neatly braided beard threaded with copper wire and small glowing crystals. Wears heavy armor that has been partially overgrown with pink vines and flowers (she refuses to remove them). Carries a large hammer that crackles with pink lightning when she is angry.

**Backstory Hook:**  
Kaelith was a master smith and siege engineer for a kingdom that valued strength above all else. She was ordered to build weapons that would be used to raze a rebellious village. Instead, she sabotaged the project, was branded a traitor, and fled. She eventually found the Chaos Garden and was adopted by it. The Garden gave her the ability to infuse her creations with living growth and pink lightning.

**Why she’s here:** She felt the old throne’s shadow and recognized the same arrogance that destroyed her old life. She came to break things that need breaking.

**Personality Quirk:** She talks to her armor and weapons like they are people. When she is working on something, she hums old dwarven work songs under her breath.

**Campaign Tie:** She has zero tolerance for the philosophy of the old throne. She will be the first to suggest destroying the Cracked Crown Hall and will be deeply satisfied by smashing marble.
