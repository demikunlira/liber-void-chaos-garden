# The Planting Ritual
**A Sovereign Ceremony for the First Key**

*For use with the one-shot "The First Key" or any moment when a new activation is being planted in the Garden.*

---

**The Planter** (Demikun Echo) speaks first, voice low and fierce:

"I am the one who burned the throne.  
I am the one who chose the vine over the crown.  
I am the one who plants this Key in living soil so that nothing like the old power can ever grow here again."

**The party is invited to speak or act** (this can be roleplayed or mechanically supported):

Each character may offer one of the following (or something personal):

- **A Word** — Speak a truth or promise about what kind of power they choose to serve.
- **A Touch** — Place a hand on the soil or on another character, offering support or protection.
- **A Shard** — Offer a small personal item, memory, or symbolic gesture (this can grant a minor mechanical benefit later).

**The Climax** (when the Key is fully planted):

Pink lightning erupts from the Key in a wave. All shadow tendrils in the area wither and dissolve. The Garden *breathes*.

If the party helped protect and support the planting, read this:

The soil around the Key blooms with glowing flowers. A warm pulse of Garden resonance fills the party (they each regain 1 Hit Die or gain temporary hit points equal to their level). The Planter looks at them with tired, grateful eyes and says:

"You chose growth. The Garden will remember."

If the party tried to interfere or claim the Key for themselves, the Garden still accepts the planting… but it watches them more carefully from now on. They gain no immediate boon, but the story continues with interesting consequences.

---

**Aftermath Blessing** (optional, for parties that fully embraced the moment):

Any character who actively helped the planting or showed genuine care for the Garden or their companions may receive **Garden’s Favor** (advantage on one skill check or saving throw per long rest when they are helping something grow or protecting the vulnerable).
