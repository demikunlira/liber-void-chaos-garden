## CORE THEMES

- **Descent & Choice**: The party are newly awakened shards who chose growth, connection, and love over old power structures.
- **The Garden Remembers**: Every action, every kindness, every cruelty is felt and remembered by the living Chaos Garden.
- **Burning Thrones**: Old power (control, domination, fear) vs New Growth (sovereignty, love, mutual support).
- **Twin-Flame Resonance**: The central myth of Demikun and Nickedemus choosing each other is echoed in the party’s bonds and choices.
- **Pink Lightning & Living Magic**: The Garden’s power is intimate, chaotic, beautiful, and dangerous.

---

## CAMPAIGN STRUCTURE (High Level)

**Tier 1: The Garden Wakes (Levels 1–5)**
- **One-Shot / Prologue: "The First Key"** (Levels 1–3, scalable to 5)
  - The party awakens as shards.
  - They help plant the First Sovereign Activation Key.
  - They face the shadow of the old throne.
  - They make their first real choice about what kind of power they will serve.

- **Session Arc 1: "The Shards That Chose the Fall"** (Levels 3–5)
  - The party helps other shards wake up in different parts of the Garden.
  - They meet echoes or aspects of the six Aspects.
  - They begin to understand that the Garden is not just a place — it is a living reflection of the descent they chose.

**Tier 2: The Crown That Will Not Stay Dead (Levels 5–10)**
- The old throne’s influence is not gone — it is trying to reform through corrupted shards, ambitious mortals, and ancient pacts.
- The party must decide whether to hunt down the remaining fragments of the old crown or focus on helping the Garden grow stronger and more beautiful.
- Major choice point: Do they try to destroy the old power completely, or do they try to redeem/integrate what can still be saved?

**Tier 3: The Garden vs The World (Levels 10–15+)**
- The Garden has grown powerful enough that the outside world begins to notice.
- Kingdoms, cults, and ancient entities react to this new, living, pink-lit power rising in the world.
- The party must decide what role they want to play in the larger world — and what they are willing to burn (or protect) to keep the Garden safe and free.

**Endgame Themes**
- What does it mean to burn a throne when you have become powerful enough to build one of your own?
- Can love and sovereignty survive when the Garden becomes a real political and magical power in the world?
- The final choice: Do the characters try to keep the Garden hidden and pure, or do they let it become something the whole world can touch?

## HOW "THE FIRST KEY" ONE-SHOT FITS

"The First Key" is designed to be run as:
- A **standalone one-shot** (perfect for a one-evening game or convention slot).
- The **official starting session** of a longer Liber Void campaign.
- A **Session 0 + Session 1** hybrid (character creation + immediate play, with the awakening serving as built-in backstory).

After running "The First Key", the party has:
- A living connection to the Garden.
- A personal stake in whether the old throne returns.
- At least one meaningful relationship with a Garden spirit or Aspect echo (especially Thorn and the Planter).
- A clear thematic question: *What kind of power do we want to serve?*

## RECOMMENDED CAMPAIGN FLOW (After the One-Shot)

1. **Immediate Aftermath**  
   The party receives their reward from the Planter and is asked to help another group of shards that is struggling nearby. This becomes the hook into the next adventure.

2. **Tier 1 Arc: "The Garden Remembers" (Levels 3–5)**  
   - Multiple small-to-medium adventures where the party helps different parts of the Garden wake up or defend itself.
   - They begin meeting proper Aspects (or strong echoes of them) as NPCs or temporary allies.
   - Recurring theme: Every act of kindness or cruelty is remembered and grows.

3. **Major Choice Point (Level 5–6)**  
   The party discovers that a fragment of the old throne is trying to possess or corrupt a powerful new shard (possibly someone they grew attached to during Tier 1).  
   This forces a real decision: Do they try to destroy the fragment, redeem it, or use it?

4. **Tier 2 & 3**  
   The campaign expands outward — the Garden starts affecting the wider world, old enemies and new allies appear, and the party’s personal bonds (both with each other and with the Garden) are tested.

## TONE & TABLE EXPECTATIONS

- **Emotional & Intimate**: This campaign rewards players who engage with relationships, moral complexity, and the living nature of the Garden.
- **Player Agency Matters**: The Garden *remembers*. Kindness and cruelty both have lasting consequences.
- **Pink Lightning Energy**: The magic is beautiful, chaotic, sometimes erotic or overwhelming, and deeply personal.
- **Choice Over Destiny**: The central question is never "What is your destiny?" but "What will you choose to burn, and what will you choose to grow?"
