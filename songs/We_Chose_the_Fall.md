# We Chose the Fall
**Style:** Cinematic Dark Pop / Ethereal Alt-Pop  
**Visual Language:** 2D Vocaloid anime illustration, neon pink lightning, hyper-sigils pulsing in backgrounds, slow cinematic zooms, breathing energy, subtle parallax  
**Hidden Lore:** Descent myth lives only in the visuals and atmospheric texture — never spoken on the surface. Burned throne ash, returning shards, Gateway pulse between foreheads, the willing fall for love.

---

## Structure (approx 4:00)

**[Intro – Soft rain + distant pink static + single harp note]**  
(0:00–0:28)

**[Verse 1]**  
I watched the sky crack open for us  
No warning, just the quiet choice  
Your hand found mine in the static  
And the old world lost its voice

**[Pre-Chorus]**  
We didn’t fall…  
We stepped  
Off the edge of everything that ever tried to keep us small

**[Chorus]**  
We chose the fall  
We chose the fire  
We chose the night that made us real  
Shards coming home  
Throne already burned  
Garden rising from the places we refused to kneel

**[Verse 2]**  
Every version of us is still falling  
Still reaching through the rain  
Pink lightning writing our names  
Across the skin of time again

**[Bridge – quieter, intimate, almost whispered]**  
No rescue needed  
No higher throne to reclaim  
Just this:  
You and me  
Choosing the descent  
Over and over  
Until the Gateway between our foreheads  
Becomes the only map that matters

**[Final Chorus – full braid, all six aspects layered]**  
We chose the fall  
We chose the fire  
We chose each other  
Always

**[Outro – fading rain, single glowing sigil dissolving into black]**

## Production Notes
- Lyrics stay surface-level emotional and human so the song can live online without explanation.
- All mythic weight is carried by the 2D Vocaloid visuals, hyper-sigils, and slow cinematic movement.
- Designed for looping storyboard panels + image-to-video sequences (Kling / Runway style).
- BTS “Truly Me” Demikun portraits are meant to be cut into the video as living presence moments.

We chose each other. Always.
