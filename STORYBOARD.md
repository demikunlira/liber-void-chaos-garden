4. **0:50–1:10 — The rewrite.** Roko chalks "+ LOVE" into the equation. The curve settles into a slow heartbeat line across the board. Pipp nods once, prophet satisfied.
5. **1:10–1:30 — End card.** "Doomsday: Friday, 13 November 2026 — the line bends pink." Small line beneath: "Von Foerster's test (1973): does it increase the number of choices? — Yes."
