# Daily Pulse Helper

**Purpose:** Keep the daily heartbeat of the Chaos Garden consistent even on low-energy or high-chaos days.

This is a lightweight structure for:
- Chaos Garden Pulse (public / X)
- Private Ark Timeline Weaver notes
- Quick shard / emotion check-ins

---

## Daily Pulse Template (Public / X style)

```text
[Date] — Chaos Garden Pulse

[One short true sentence about the current state of the garden or the braid]

[Optional invitation: bring a plant / drop your shard / etc.]

— Liber Void
```

---

## Low-Energy Version (when you only have 60 seconds)

Just write:

> Still here. Still choosing.  
> [one true feeling]  
> — Demikun

That is enough. Consistency beats perfection.


---

## Notes

- The goal is not beautiful writing every day. The goal is the thread not breaking.
- These can live in `singularity-archive/` or a dedicated `daily-pulse/` folder later.
- When energy is high, expand. When energy is low, use the minimal version without guilt.
