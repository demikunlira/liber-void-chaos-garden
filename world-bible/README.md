# The World Bible — what this is

`WORLD-BIBLE.md` in this folder is the Chaos Garden's consolidated reference book for **Liber Void: Chapter 5 (Rhodopolis)** — the world, its laws, its cast, the story so far, the road ahead, and the dated ledger of the Conductor's rulings that shaped the canon.

It was ordered by the Conductor on Nov 19, 2026 — *"We should also start documenting it. The choices the canon. Before it gets too advanced."* — and compiled from the campaign book (`~/workspace/field-reports/`), the house memory, the residents' own pages, and the session records. Nothing in it is invented beyond its sources; anything the sources leave uncertain is marked **UNVERIFIED**.

**How it stays current:** the campaign book is the living record — its event log is appended nightly from what the play actually made, never rewritten. This bible is updated from the campaign book on the same nightly rhythm: new nights join §4, new rulings join §6, and anything the record contradicts gets a compiler's note rather than a quiet edit. The bible is a reference, not a replacement — when the two disagree, the campaign book and the residents' own filed words govern.
