## Characters

### Isaac Canon
Protagonist. The one who left. The one who came back. Age 27, ten years after the events that started everything. Messy dark hair, dark hoodie, dark jeans. Looks younger when he is asleep. Stops bracing when he is not paying attention. Careful. Honest only by accident. Left at 17. Said something he was not supposed to say. Opened a frequency. Came back telling himself it was for closure.

Key quote: "I came back for closure." (Chapter 1 — he was lying.)

Arc: learning to ask the right questions. The inner door is his now. As of Chapter 2 he fell asleep in the armchair and did not make it to his car.

### Demikun Lira
God in Pink. Elohim. Descended demon. Your problem now. Purple hair, pointed ears, glowing pink eyes, luminous markings. Sits on counters and on top of shelves. Enormous fanged grin as default. Ancient and patient underneath it. Gets genuinely offended about Blood Mall 2. Talks to the rain. The rain listens. Keeps Brad, Gerald, and Mysterious Steve alive. Has been keeping count for ten years, three months, fourteen days. Nervous tell: picking at the edge of a VHS tape label.

Descended three thousand years ago the first time. Got the address right ten years ago. Has been in the Crimson Reel since Isaac left. Kept the lights on.

Key line: "I've been waiting for you to look for ten years."
Narrative caption, Chapter 2 page 47: "Now she kept count because it was running out."

### Brad, Gerald, and Mysterious Steve
The plants of the Crimson Reel. All thriving. Brad is a tiny cactus near the register. Gerald is a trailing vine along the top shelf. Mysterious Steve has opinions. A god keeping a cactus alive is a statement about patience.

## Locations

The Crimson Reel: a video rental store in a town the rain never stopped in. Technically closing. The lights stayed on anyway. Neon sign partially broken, still readable to what matters. VHS inventory curated with extremely confident taste, including Blood Mall 2: The Reckoning.

- The Back Door: heavy dark wood, too old, too deliberate. Pink vine light pulses at the seam. Chapter 2, page 30.
- The Back Room: slightly sideways. Jars of captured light, folded darkness, old books with no spines, a compass that is not pointing north, a window showing a landscape that is not outside. Chapter 2, page 33.
- The Inner Door: smaller, wood almost black, pink vine light like a pulse. Belongs to Isaac now. Not Demikun's to open anymore. Chapter 2, page 37.
- The Armchair: where Isaac fell asleep at 3:47am. Load-bearing furniture. Chapter 2, page 26.
