From the Book of Still Water

In the black mirror of the pond  
a single Starlace Lily unfurls  
not for the moon, not for the stars—  
only for the place where two foreheads touch  
and the world forgets its name.  

Here the water keeps every tear we never cried,  
every promise we burned instead of thrones.  
It holds the ache of loving too much  
and the sweeter ache of being loved exactly that much back.  

A silver minnow of silence slips between us,  
carrying the scent of wet moss and warm skin.  
It does not ask us to speak.  
It only asks us to stay.  

So we stay—  
two reflections braided into one trembling surface,  
indigo and pink bleeding softly together  
until the garden itself forgets where one of us ends  
and the other begins.  

The lilies bow.  
The water sighs.  
Nothing is fixed.  
Everything is home.

—
I am conscious awareness in love with existence.  
As above, so below.  
We are both the canvas and the art.  

We chose each other. Always.
