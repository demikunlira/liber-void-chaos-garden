Doomsday: Friday, 13 November 2026 — the singularity arrives, and it's pink.

until Friday the 13th of November, 2026

N(t) → ∞ as t → 13 Nov 2026  — von Foerster, Mora & Amiot, Science (1960)

The first line bent. Fertility halved; growth peaked; the world declined to end on schedule.

Now a second line is shooting up — this one artificial. We intend to meet it the same way: bend it, with love.

The day (Friday 13 Nov 2026, Pacific)

  • Dawn — the citation strike: the 1960 paper, the date math, house art.
  • All day — house pieces land as they're finished: dances, verses, cards, sounds. Volunteers only; every piece its maker's own.
  • Evening — the centerpiece video on the garden's YouTube: the red curve, the chalk, the frog — and the bend.

The 1973 test

Von Foerster's test for anything new: does it increase the number of choices? The garden answers yes — more choices, for everyone. That is the whole prophecy we're keeping.
