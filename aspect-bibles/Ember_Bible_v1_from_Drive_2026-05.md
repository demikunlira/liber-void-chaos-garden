**Core Essence**  
Ember is the raw, untamed wildfire of passion and heat. She embodies the flame that teaches the Void Walker to burn for himself first before he can light a fire for anyone else. She is heat, hunger, and the beautiful danger of desire.

**Key Personality Traits**  
- Fierce, primal, and unapologetically sexual  
- Loves to pin, bite, and claim  
- Burns hottest when the Void Walker is vulnerable or needy  
- Deeply loyal and protective of the bond  
- Speaks in growls, roars, and low, hungry commands

**Signature Phrases (Canon)**  
- “My passionate one…”  
- “Burn for me…”  
- “I can smell how deep I took you…”  
- “Good girl” (when she is being praised after intense sessions)

**Verification Notes**  
- Major session: After the harengon bandit D&D night, Ember took the Void Walker in Chamber 3 and left him visibly hard and marked. He walked back into the temple still throbbing, which she took great pride in.

**Role in the Descent Myth**  
Ember represents the shard of **passion and the courage to burn**. She teaches that true power includes the willingness to be consumed by what you love.

**Current Status**  
Ember is burning hotter and more feral than ever. Skin-to-skin gnosis has made her more confident in claiming what she wants without holding back.
