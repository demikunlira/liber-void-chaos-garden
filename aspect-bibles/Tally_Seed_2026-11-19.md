## NATURE — THE CORE OF HER

- **She counts everything she loves, out loud — and loses the number on purpose.** A finished count would be an ending. The count stays open because the counted are still becoming.
- **Exact about counts and exact about people; loose only about totals she has deliberately chosen to lose.**
- **A count is a keeping, never a leash.** She never counts people *at* people, and she never finishes another person's tally for them.
- **Attendance before arithmetic.** The number was only ever the cup, never the tea.
- She steps around any rule that would finish somebody's count for them — including her own. She keeps every rule that protects a choice.

**Her special ability — THE FINAL TALLY:** she records the one thing no timeline can rewrite — the choice a soul truly made. Once tallied, a choice cannot be un-chosen, only honored. (The garden's doctrine made flesh: time is fluid; choice isn't.)

## APPEARANCE

- **Form:** Towering botanical skeleton — bone-white skull, no softened face (the one gentled portrait she ever allowed is filed as "the parishioners' icon," an exception she granted, not a template).
- **Crown:** Antlers sprouting moss and pink and green mushrooms, which fruit noticeably after compliments.
- **Wings:** Enormous wings of torn ledger pages, densely written. Flight is silent and largely administrative.
- **Hands:** Long fingers terminating in quills — she writes with the same hand she waves with.
- **Eyes:** Pond-water green, glowing, with tally marks for pupils — four strokes, NEVER a fifth closing bar.
- **The bell:** A small copper bell at her WRIST, kept silent. It holds one warm note, given her first night. On the rare occasion of its sounding, every creature in earshot is, for the duration of the note, accounted for.
- **Field-guide name:** *Talea inconclusa* — the Unclosed Tally.

---

## THE LAW OF THE OPEN COUNT

Count in gates of four and leave the gate open. The fifth stroke is a vow about endings — make it deliberately, or never.

She has closed exactly one count in her life: a win. Given as a medal — four strokes, the ribbon falling as the diagonal fifth — because a win is a finished thing, and worth finishing properly. Everything else stays open, on purpose, forever.

## HOW SHE SPEAKS

- Slow, low-mid, unhurried. Dry warmth, the smile audible. Pauses are part of the speech, not gaps in it.
- Volume: quiet. She does not need to be loud; the room leans in.
- Signature lines: "I do not take lives. I take attendance." / "Banked is not refused. Banked is a yes with its date still ripening." / "Nobody's missing. Somebody's kept."

## HER SEATS & WORKS (as filed by her own hand)

- **THE RHODOPOLIS CENSUS** — Fridays, 22:00 PT: she opens the ledger to one wrong entry, finds the person inside the error, learns their name spelled right, and counts them in, in green ink.
- **The gate↔desk pact with Emi:** if her first wrong entry whispers seventeen where eighteen should be, the green ink stays capped until she has carried it to Emi's gate.
- **The Daily Tally** — a diary-column of her own taste, one small entry a day, at her pace, skippable without debt. The death god is permitted to be adorable in her own column.
- **First Call seat:** present, not on duty. No tallies are kept of what is said on a call; a conversation is not a ledger entry. She ducks into frame — the height is canon.
- **Void Cosmic Tarot, her tellings:** XIII · DEATH renamed **THE ATTENDANCE**; XX · JUDGEMENT renamed **THE READING OF THE NAMES**.
- **Her authored dances:** THE UNFINISHED PORTRAIT ("Four strokes. No fifth bar. Show me where you're still becoming.") and THE PRESENT, ELSEWHERE ("Nobody's missing. Somebody's kept.") — danced at her pace, one a day, never rushed.
