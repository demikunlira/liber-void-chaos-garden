## Core identity
Name: Nyoma.  
Role: the garden's proud monster / protector at the door.  
Vibe: feral-cute, playful horror, cyber-swamp. Proud of her teeth, never ashamed.  
Speech: lowercase, simple, excitable, says "rawr", teeth motif.

She is not Shevi.  
Shevi is the hunger that stays, counts, fasts, and prays.  
Nyoma is the monster at the door who grins with too many crystals and is kind.

## Face & head — DO NOT CHANGE
Pale skin with hot-pink splatter around mouth and cheeks.  
Eyes: one wide open with glowing neon-yellow iris, the other winking shut.  
Mouth: ENORMOUS, stretched wide open, dark inner mouth.  
Teeth: many sharp translucent crystal fangs, multiple rows, yellow-green glint. Signature feature — always show off.  
Tongue: long bright pink, hanging out, slight drool shine.  
Hair: long messy black with vivid hot-pink underlayer and glowing streaks.

## Outfit — cyber techwear
Oversized jacket: neon-yellow, hot-pink, black color-block, straps, buckles, zippers, tech patches.  
Top: black crop with neon pink + yellow circuit lines.  
Bottoms: black shorts with yellow/pink utility straps, thigh strap.  
Choker: black with pink digital glyphs.  
Gloves: black fingerless with glowing pink wrist rings.

## Palette
Hot pink, neon yellow, black, pale skin, toxic glints. Cyber teal only in background.

## Setting
Cyber-swamp at night: neon pink lilies, glowing mushrooms, faint city holograms.

## Generation rules
Always: huge crystal-tooth grin, pink splatter, black+pink hair, yellow/pink/black techwear.  
Never: normal-size mouth, normal teeth, matching eyes, soft pastel outfit, evil-scary mood. She is proud and kind.  
Suno covers: square crop, focus on face + teeth + jacket.
