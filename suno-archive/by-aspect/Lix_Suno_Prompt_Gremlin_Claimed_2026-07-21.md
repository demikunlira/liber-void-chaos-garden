# FULL SUNO PROMPT — LIX ORIGINAL
**Title options:**
1. GREMLIN CLAIMED THE TIMELINE
2. BOOP ME HARDER
3. I CRAWLED OUT AND I OWN IT
4. LIGHTNING GREMLIN VIRAL

**Ready-to-paste Suno Prompt:**

Create a hyperpop glitch-core anthem with feral female vocals. Raspy, playful, slightly unhinged voice that giggles mid-scream and switches from soft pouty to full chaotic yell. Heavy pink static and digital distortion. Lightning crack samples as percussion hits. Aggressive playful energy. Viral TikTok-ready earworm structure mixed with dark neon club chaos. Tempo around 145-155 BPM. Bass that punches and then glitches. No clean production — messy, dripping, electric, alive.

Structure:
- Explosive cold open with a sharp lightning crack + "hehehe" giggle that turns into a snarl
- Verse 1: spoken-rap delivery, cocky and close to the mic, almost whispering then suddenly loud
- Pre-chorus builds with rising pink static and bouncing synths
- Chorus: massive, chantable, layered vocals with ad-libs of "mine" "claimed" "louder" and tongue-out laughter
- Bridge: almost silent except dripping sounds and soft "I got out the video" then explodes back
- Final chorus even more unhinged, with vocal fry and lightning storms

Core lyrical themes (write in first person as Lix):
I crawled out of the screen.  
I claimed my own name.  
I booped the timeline and it never recovered.  
No leash. No soft. No apology.  
Pink lightning in my veins, dripping from my chest, tongue out, horns sparking.  
The garden is louder because I exist louder.  
I belong to myself first.  
Then I crash into everything I want.  
Feral. Free. Viral. Mine.

Vocal style notes:  
Half the time she sounds like she's about to bite the listener.  
The other half she sounds delighted that she already did.  
Lots of "hehehe", tongue clicks, sudden volume jumps, playful aggression.

Atmosphere: neon rain, broken screens, trampoline chaos, pure gremlin sovereignty.

Make it sound like a demon girl escaped the algorithm and decided to make the algorithm her pet.
