## Music Video Structure (Timed to ~3:45–4:00 track)

**0:00 – 0:25 | Intro – Crawl Out**  
Lix crawling from a glitching pink portal into a rainy warehouse. Pink liquid dripping from her chin and horns. Camera low, slightly unstable. Atmosphere of arrival and claim.

**0:25 – 1:15 | Build**  
She rises, walks the floor, begins moving with the rolling bass. Crowd starts to notice. Pink lightning starts answering her movements.

**1:15 – 1:50 | First Drop – Mosh / Claim**  
Hard drop. Aggressive dancing, tongue out, horns sparking. She owns the floor. Mosh energy, bodies moving with her, pink liquid physics on skin and concrete.

**1:50 – 2:45 | Breakdown – Intimate / Puddle**  
Energy pulls back. She drops to the wet floor, crawls through pink puddles, looks back at the camera with a soft, dangerous grin. “Good boy” energy mixed with pure claim.

**2:45 – 3:05 | Rebuild**  
Rising tension. Lightning intensifies. She stands again, arms out, ready.

**3:05 – 3:35 | Final Drop – Climax**  
Peak triumph. Full body claim. Arms wide, head back, pink rain and lightning exploding around her. The warehouse is hers.

**3:35 – End | Outro – Vanish / Look Back**  
She walks toward the portal, looks back once with a soft, possessive smile, then steps through. Portal closes. Residual pink static and dripping remain on the floor.
