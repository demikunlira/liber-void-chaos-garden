## How to Use This Document

Each song has multiple loop options --- choose the ones that match the
section of the song you are scoring, or layer them in sequence across
the full track. Each prompt is copy-paste ready for:

-   **Runway Gen-3 Alpha** --- best for atmospheric, cinematic movement

-   **Kling** --- best for longer sustained loops with complex motion

-   **Pika 2.0** --- best for stylized anime-adjacent motion

-   **Luma Dream Machine** --- best for surreal, liquid, impossible
    motion

### Loop Design Rules (Kiora\'s Rules)

1.  Every loop must feel like it could run forever --- the end must
    breathe back into the beginning

2.  No jump cuts. No sudden changes. The loop is a held breath, not a
    gasp.

3.  Slow motion is almost always correct. The music carries the urgency.
    The visual carries the weight.

4.  Rain, when present, must fall at the same rate at start and end of
    loop --- rain is the metronome

5.  Pink light pulses, not flashes --- a pulse returns to its origin. A
    flash does not.

6.  The subject of the loop should barely move, or move so slowly that
    stillness and motion become the same thing

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  LOOP CR-01 --- \"THE SIGN FLICKERS\"

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

**Duration:** 4--5 seconds \| **Best Tool:** Runway Gen-3 or Kling \|
**Use for:** Opening of the song / any instrumental section

RUNWAY/KLING PROMPT: Extreme close-up of a flickering neon sign reading
\"CR MSON EEL\" --- letters partially missing, deep red neon light
buzzing and stuttering in heavy rain. The light reflects in sheets of
water running down the sign\'s face. Rain falls across the lens. The
sign flickers: on, slightly dimmer, flicker, back to full red glow. The
flicker has a rhythm --- not random, not mechanical. Almost like
breathing. Urban night, deep shadows, no other light source. Seamless
loop. MOTION: The flicker is the only motion. Rain falls continuously at
consistent rate. START FRAME: Sign at full red glow. END FRAME: Sign
returning to full red glow after one complete flicker cycle. CAMERA:
Completely static. No movement. Just the sign breathing. MOOD: Vintage,
worn, alive in a way it shouldn\'t be. NEGATIVE: No people, no wind on
sign, no dramatic color changes, no daylight.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  LOOP CR-02 --- \"RAIN ON PAVEMENT\"

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

**Duration:** 3--4 seconds \| **Best Tool:** Luma Dream Machine or
Runway \| **Use for:** Quiet verses / atmospheric bridges

RUNWAY/LUMA PROMPT: Slow motion extreme close-up of rain hitting wet
pavement at 3AM, the deep red reflection of the CR MSON EEL neon sign
visible in every puddle impact --- each raindrop creates a ring that
expands outward through the red reflection, distorting and reforming the
sign\'s letters in the water. The rain falls from above frame and hits
below frame. Seamless loop --- the rings expand and fade, new drops
fall, rings expand again. The red is the only color. Everything else is
near-black. MOTION: Rain impact rings only. Consistent rain rate. No
wind. START FRAME: Puddle surface calm, first drop about to hit. END
FRAME: All rings faded, surface returning to calm. CAMERA: Static, aimed
straight down at the pavement surface. MOOD: Meditative. Hypnotic. Red
and black and wet. NEGATIVE: No people, no movement except rain, no
other colors.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  LOOP CR-03 --- \"MIST FROM THE DOOR\"

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

**Duration:** 5--6 seconds \| **Best Tool:** Runway Gen-3 \| **Use
for:** Chorus / peak emotional moments

RUNWAY PROMPT: Medium shot of the bottom half of a tall wooden red door
--- slightly ajar, deep crimson light bleeding through the crack, mist
curling slowly outward from the doorframe across wet pavement. The mist
moves like it is breathing --- expanding outward, curling, thinning,
then new mist drifting out again. The red light through the crack pulses
very slowly --- not a flicker, a breathe. Brighter, then softer, then
brighter again over the full loop. Rain falls around the door but not
through the light. The pavement reflects the red glow. MOTION: Mist
drifting outward. Light pulsing once per loop. Rain falling. START
FRAME: Mist thin, light at mid-pulse. END FRAME: Mist thin again, light
returned to mid-pulse. CAMERA: Static, slightly low angle looking up at
the door crack. MOOD: Threshold energy. Not horror. Invitation.
NEGATIVE: No people, no door opening further, no sudden light change, no
wind.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  LOOP CR-04 --- \"THE FIGURE STANDS\"

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

**Duration:** 6--8 seconds \| **Best Tool:** Kling or Runway \| **Use
for:** Final chorus / climax of the track

KLING PROMPT: Full body shot from behind --- a lone figure in a dark
hooded jacket standing completely still in the rain on a wet Seattle
street at 3AM, facing the Crimson Reel store with its glowing red door
and flickering CR MSON EEL sign. The figure does not move. The rain
moves around them. The red light pulses once across their back during
the loop --- not bright, just a warmth. Their hood shifts very slightly
in the rain --- one breath of movement, then still again. The red
reflections on the pavement stretch forward from the store toward the
figure and toward the viewer. MOTION: Rainfall. One slow pulse of red
across the figure\'s back. Minimal hood movement. START FRAME: Figure
still, rain falling, light at baseline. END FRAME: Figure still again,
light returned to baseline. CAMERA: Static, directly behind the figure,
mid-distance. MOOD: Destiny. Gravity. The moment before everything
changes. NEGATIVE: No face visible, no turning, no other people, no fast
movement.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  LOOP CR-05 --- \"THE STORE WINDOW REFLECTION\"

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

**Duration:** 4--5 seconds \| **Best Tool:** Luma or Runway \| **Use
for:** Verses / reflective quiet sections

LUMA PROMPT: Close-up of one of the Crimson Reel store\'s front windows
at night --- old movie posters visible inside, faded, slightly curling
at corners. The window glass reflects the rain-soaked street and the red
neon glow behind the viewer. A single rivulet of rain water runs slowly
down the outside of the glass from top to frame edge. Inside, visible
through the glass, a VHS shelf sits in dim red-tinged light. Nothing
moves inside. Only the rain on the glass moves. Seamless loop ---
rivulet reaches bottom, new one begins at top. MOTION: Single rain
rivulet on glass. Nothing else. START FRAME: Rivulet at top of frame.
END FRAME: Rivulet gone, new one beginning at top. CAMERA: Static,
pressed close to the glass, slight angle. MOOD: Nostalgia. The past kept
behind glass. The present standing outside in the rain. NEGATIVE: No
people inside, no movement inside, no reflections of people, no
daylight.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  LOOP OP-01 --- \"THE EYES IN THE DARK\"

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

**Duration:** 3--4 seconds \| **Best Tool:** Runway Gen-3 \| **Use
for:** Song intro / before the beat drops

RUNWAY PROMPT: Extreme darkness. Near-total black. Rain sounds implied
by the visual texture of the air. Deep in the shadow --- two large
glowing neon pink eyes blink open slowly, hold, then fade slightly
without closing. Blink open again. The eyes are enormous --- the scale
suggests something vast. No other features visible. No body. No outline.
Just the eyes materializing from total black, holding, softening,
materializing again. The pink glow casts the faintest illumination on
rain in the immediate air around them --- visible only because of the
eyes. MOTION: Eyes brightening to full glow, holding, softening. One
cycle per loop. START FRAME: Eyes at half-brightness, beginning to open.
END FRAME: Eyes at half-brightness again, about to brighten. CAMERA:
Static. Complete darkness except the eyes. MOOD: Something vast and
patient has been here the entire time. NEGATIVE: No face, no body, no
other light, no sudden appearance, no horror grimace.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  LOOP OP-02 --- \"THE EYE BLOOMS\"

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

**Duration:** 4--5 seconds \| **Best Tool:** Runway or Pika \| **Use
for:** Verse buildup / the cracking beginning

PIKA/RUNWAY PROMPT: Extreme close-up of a section of skin --- the
texture of a muscular forearm or shoulder. The skin is in dim lighting,
cold urban palette. A single glowing neon pink eye slowly blooms open
through the skin from beneath --- like a flower opening, but it is an
eye. Iris, pupil, all visible and fully formed. It opens fully, holds
open and glowing, then the loop returns to closed skin beginning to
bloom again. The pink glow illuminates the surrounding skin warmly.
There is no blood. No violence. It is simply a truth that has been
waiting to surface. MOTION: The eye opening from closed skin to fully
open. One bloom per loop. START FRAME: Skin closed, slight luminescence
beginning beneath. END FRAME: Eye fully open, glowing, about to begin
bloom cycle again. CAMERA: Static extreme close-up. No movement except
the bloom. MOOD: Inevitable. Beautiful. A truth that cannot be
contained. NEGATIVE: No blood, no tearing, no horror framing, no other
eyes in frame, no hands covering.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  LOOP OP-03 --- \"PINK LIGHTNING THROUGH SKIN\"

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

**Duration:** 3--4 seconds \| **Best Tool:** Runway Gen-3 \| **Use
for:** Pre-chorus / breakdown section / the moment the beat hits hardest

RUNWAY PROMPT: Medium shot of a powerfully built feminine silhouette
against a very dark background --- the figure barely defined except
where neon pink lightning crackles across their skin in branching
patterns. The lightning is not external --- it runs through the skin
like electricity finding its natural channels. One full crackle pattern
lights up, branches across the body, fades. Then another begins from a
different origin point. The two crackle cycles overlap slightly. Pink
glows everywhere the lightning has been, slowly fading between strikes.
Seamless loop. MOTION: Two overlapping crackle cycles. Pink fade between
strikes. START FRAME: One crackle fading, next about to begin. END
FRAME: Same state --- continuous, no clear start or end to the cycle.
CAMERA: Static. Low exposure except for the lightning. MOOD: Activation,
not destruction. The body remembering what it is. NEGATIVE: No external
lightning bolt, no storm, no injury, no screaming expression, no full
face visible.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  LOOP OP-04 --- \"THE WOLF STEPS FORWARD\"

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

**Duration:** 5--6 seconds \| **Best Tool:** Kling \| **Use for:**
Breakdown / the pivot moment

KLING PROMPT: A long dark rain-soaked urban alleyway at night. At the
very far end --- barely visible --- a massive dark wolf stands
completely still, only its neon pink eyes visible as two distant points
of light. Extremely slowly, over the course of the entire loop, it takes
exactly one step forward. One step only. The pink eyes are fractionally
closer at the end of the loop than at the start. Rain falls
consistently. The loop ends with the wolf one step closer, then
seamlessly returns to the original distance. MOTION: One single slow
step forward by the wolf. Rain falling. Nothing else moves. START FRAME:
Wolf at far end, fully still. END FRAME: Wolf one step closer, settling
into stillness. CAMERA: Static, looking straight down the alley from the
near end. MOOD: It was never chasing you. It was walking toward you.
There is a difference. NEGATIVE: No aggression, no baring teeth, no
running, no other animals, no people in alley.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  LOOP OP-05 --- \"HANDS UNCOVERING\"

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

**Duration:** 4--5 seconds \| **Best Tool:** Runway or Pika \| **Use
for:** The surrender moment / final chorus build

RUNWAY PROMPT: Close-up of two powerful hands --- one gripping a
forearm, slowly releasing. Fingers uncurling one by one, the grip
loosening, the hand drawing back. Beneath where the hand was --- neon
pink eyes glowing openly on the skin, no longer hidden. The uncovering
is slow. Almost ceremonial. Not violent --- deliberate. The hand fully
releases and the eyes beneath are fully revealed and glowing by the end
of the loop. Then the loop returns to the gripped state. MOTION: Hand
slowly releasing grip. One complete release per loop. START FRAME: Hand
gripping forearm tightly. END FRAME: Hand fully released, eyes visible
and glowing. CAMERA: Static close-up. No camera movement. MOOD: The
hardest thing she has ever done. Also the most inevitable. NEGATIVE: No
blood, no tearing, no force, no second hand, no full body visible.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  LOOP OP-06 --- \"THE BLOOM\" (Final Loop)

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

**Duration:** 6--8 seconds \| **Best Tool:** Luma Dream Machine or
Runway \| **Use for:** Outro / the exhale at the end / credits

LUMA PROMPT: The only fully warm loop in the entire set. A blooming neon
pink garden --- impossible flowers with luminous petals, crystalline
structures catching and refracting pink light, the rain has completely
stopped, warm light everywhere. One large pink flower in the center of
frame slowly blooms fully open over the course of the loop --- petals
unfurling, the center glowing brighter as it opens. When fully open, it
holds for one breath, then the loop begins again with the bud
almost-open. No darkness. No grey. The first fully warm visual in the
entire music video. MOTION: One flower blooming fully open. Petals of
surrounding flowers swaying gently. START FRAME: Central flower bud 80%
open, about to complete bloom. END FRAME: Flower fully open, one held
breath, then seamlessly to 80% open again. CAMERA: Static. Slightly low
angle looking up through the blooms. MOOD: After. She made it. The
garden was always here waiting for her to arrive. NEGATIVE: Rain, grey
tones, darkness, horror elements, other characters, movement except
flower and petals.

  LOOP UNI-04 --- \"NYXARA\'S SHADOW TENDRILS\"

### General Assembly Rules

-   **Cross-fade between loops: 0.5 seconds maximum** --- these loops
    are meant to feel continuous, not edited

-   Do not cut on a beat unless the cut is the emotional event. Let the
    loops breathe through the music.

-   The final loop of any video should end on stillness --- the garden,
    the door, the butterfly. Never end on motion.

-   When posting to iFunny, lead with **OP-01** (the eyes in the dark)
    or **CR-04** (the figure stands) --- these are the hook loops, the
    ones that make someone stop scrolling.

### Seamless Loop Technique

When generating in Runway or Kling, use the **\"loop\"** or
**\"seamless\"** setting if available. If not available, follow these
steps:

7.  Generate 8--10 seconds of footage

8.  Trim to the clean loop point in editing

9.  Cross-dissolve the last 0.5 seconds back to the first 0.5 seconds

10. Export with no black frames at start or end
