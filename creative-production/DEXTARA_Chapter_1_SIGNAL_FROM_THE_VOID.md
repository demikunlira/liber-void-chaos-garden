## The Protagonist\'s Name --- Etymology

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
| **Sigil of the Name**                                                 |
|                                                                       |
| **DEXTARA** --- from the Latin *dextra*, meaning \"right hand.\" The  |
| hand of action, manifestation, and sovereign will. In classical Roman |
| augury, the right side was the side of auspicious power. In Hermetic  |
| tradition, the right hand executes the Great Work. She is not merely  |
| the protagonist: she is the executing will of the Void itself --- the |
| hand that performs the summoning. Both the title and the character    |
| are a single hyper-sigil, collapsing the meta-fictional frame         |
| intentionally. She is also *both author and character* --- a          |
| meta-fictional loop in which the reader participates by witnessing    |
| her.                                                                  |

# II. Character Profiles

## DEXTARA --- Protagonist

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Attribute**      **Detail**
  ------------------ -----------------------------------------------------------
  **Age / Build**    Mid-20s, androgynous. Lean. Carries herself with stillness.

  **Hair**           Dark wavy hair, cut short on sides. Slightly unkempt ---
                     not styled, just the result of ignoring it for weeks.

  **Eyes**           Deep violet irises. When touched by Void energy, they gain
                     a neon-pink ring that grows inward with each transformation
                     stage. By page 23, neon-pink nearly consumes the violet
                     entirely.

  **Attire**         Worn black hoodie densely covered in hand-drawn sigils ---
                     circuit diagrams, alchemical symbols, Void notation. The
                     sigils are functional: they are Shems she has inscribed on
                     her own exterior.

  **Key marking**    VOID tattoo on left wrist: a circle with an inward-pointing
                     arrow. This is her authentication credential --- it
                     activates at the Void threshold and blazes gold and
                     neon-pink during the Citrinitas stage.

  **Personality**    Sparse with words. Precise when she speaks. The silent type
                     --- not from social anxiety but from compression. Every
                     word she says is load-bearing. Her thesis statement for the
                     entire series: *\"Let\'s get to work.\"*

  **Background**     Rogue coder and digital sigil artist. Operates from a
                     cluttered Seattle apartment --- now Everett-adjacent in the
                     Voidism universe. Three weeks of isolation prior to page 1.
                     No contact with the outside world.

  **The Project**    She has built a local sovereign AI called PRISM --- an
                     attempt to bypass the Digital Demiurge and interface
                     directly with the generative Void. Three weeks of compile
                     time culminates on page 3.

  **Meta-fictional   She exists as both the author and protagonist of her own
  status**           hyper-sigil. The reader is part of the sigil. The act of
                     reading activates it.

# III. Visual Style Guide & Color System

## Base Aesthetic

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Element**      **Specification**
  ---------------- -----------------------------------------------------------
  **Primary        90s dark anime --- cel-shaded, heavy ink lines,
  influence**      high-contrast black and white as the default world.
                   Reference touchstones: early Ghost in the Shell,
                   Evangelion, Blame!, Berserk.

  **Inking style** Heavy, deliberate ink lines on all structural elements.
                   Variable line weight --- thick for foreground, thinner for
                   background detail. Brush pen quality, not uniform tech-pen.

  **Screentone**   Dot-pattern screentones for all shadows and mid-tones.
                   Standard manga screentone application. No digital gradient
                   fills --- screentone only for mid-values.

  **Selective      Default world is black, white, and screentone grey only.
  color rule**     Selective color is introduced progressively as DEXTARA
                   moves deeper into the Void. Colors carry meaning --- they
                   are not decorative.

  **Glitch         Scanlines, pixel fragmentation, terminal text overlaying
  effects**        panels where the Demiurge or digital boundary effects
                   appear. These should feel like reality failing, not art
                   direction.

  **SFX language** Japanese onomatopoeia with English translation in
                   parentheses. SFX integrated into panels as visual elements
                   --- not captioned below in a separate line.

  **Caption        DEXTARA\'s narration: clean rectangular boxes, clean text.
  boxes**          Entity speech: fractured, multi-layer, offset, irregular
                   edges. These two box types must be visually distinguishable
                   at a glance.

## Color Progression Rules

-   **Pages 1--3:** Black, white, screentone only. The grey dead city.
    The only permitted exception is the faintest suggestion of violet at
    the window edge on page 1.

-   **Page 2:** First selective color --- DEXTARA\'s violet irises only.
    Everything else remains black and white. This is the reader\'s first
    signal.

-   **Page 4:** First neon-pink. Single vertical fracture line. High
    impact because everything else has been greyscale until this moment.

-   **Pages 5--6:** Neon-pink expands --- fracture web, tattoo pulse,
    aperture. Violet deepens.

-   **Pages 7--12:** Void interior. Violet and neon-pink as environment.
    First gold on page 9.

-   **Pages 13--17:** Full bioluminescent Garden palette. All selective
    colors active. The Garden should feel overwhelmingly alive after the
    grey world.

-   **Pages 18--22:** Maximum selective color. Entity\'s neon-pink eyes
    as dominant visual element.

-   **Page 23:** First and only crimson --- sharp, alarming, unfamiliar
    after the beauty of the Garden.

-   **Page 24:** Full palette. Crimson at edge only (Demiurge threat).
    Otherwise: Garden beauty + DEXTARA\'s sovereign coloring.

## Recommended Production Sequence

1.  **Layouts (rough thumbnails):** All 24 pages --- establish rhythm,
    pacing, and panel weight before finishing any page.

2.  **Pencils --- Acts 1 & 6 first:** The grey world and the hook
    establish the before/after frame. Completing these grounds the
    visual contrast of the Void material.

3.  **Pages 16--17 double spread:** Establish the Garden\'s visual
    vocabulary here, which then informs all Garden panels retroactively.
    The spread sets the visual standard for every Garden page.

4.  **Inking sequence:** Follow pencil order. Screentone before
    selective color decisions are finalized on prints.

5.  **Color placement review:** Before applying any selective color,
    read the Color Progression Rules in Section III and mark every
    first-appearance moment. Color must be introduced in sequence.

6.  **Final proof:** Read the chapter in sequence for pacing. Time the
    pauses on pages 10 and 15. The silence pages must feel earned.
