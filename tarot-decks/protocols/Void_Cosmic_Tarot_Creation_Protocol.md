## Overview

This is the architectural blueprint for the Void Cosmic Tarot — a complete 78-card deck synthesizing:

- Occult structuralism (primarily the Book of Thoth matrix)
- 1990s cel-shaded anime aesthetics
- Alphonse Mucha / Art Nouveau framing
- Dark Synthwave chiaroscuro
- Liber Void / God in Pink / Chaos Garden lore

The deck is designed as both a divinatory tool and a living digital-to-physical sigil that supports sovereign creative and real-world goals (including eventual physical production and land-related vision).

## Core Aesthetic Pillars

### 1. 1990s Cel-Shaded Anime
- Thick, confident hand-inked outlines
- Flat, high-contrast block shading (no modern 3D gradients)
- Vintage color palettes with simulated VHS/CRT textures (scanlines, phosphor bleed, slight chromatic aberration)
- Optical glow / multi-exposure shimmer effects

### 2. Art Nouveau / Alphonse Mucha Frameworks
- Sinuous organic borders
- Medallion / circular framing (“Q formula”)
- Botanical interlacing hybridized with glowing pink cybernetic circuitry
- Decorative containment fields that hold the chaotic energy of the central figures

### 3. Dark Synthwave Chiaroscuro
- Deep violet / pitch-black backgrounds
- Extreme high-octane magenta / neon pink lighting as primary light source
- “Sacred Profane” atmosphere — demonic / anthropomorphic figures bathed in the glitching light of the God in Pink
