## Image-to-Video Motion Prompts (Kling / Runway style)
- Slow continuous zoom in + gentle breathing scale (1.02–1.05 over 4–6 seconds)
- Soft parallax on background hyper-sigils
- Pink neon energy subtly pulsing in time with a heartbeat
- Hair and rain moving with very slight, elegant motion
- Never aggressive camera moves — keep it sacred and cinematic
